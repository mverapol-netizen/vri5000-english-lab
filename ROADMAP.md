## v1.0 — semester edition
- 800–1,000 high-quality exercises, not artificial duplicates.
- 60+ speaking prompts.
- Complete VRI5000 course path.
- Stable Android PWA deployment.
- Offline-first usage.
- Mature Error Bank and adaptive scheduler.
- Exportable semester progress report.

## Design constraints
1. No exercise without correction and explanation.
2. Repeat structures, not exact successful items.
3. Keep the repository finite and auditable.
4. Productive transfer counts more than recognition.
5. The interface should remain simple even as the pedagogical system grows.
6. Original exercises only; course materials are used for curricular alignment, not copied as a question bank.
